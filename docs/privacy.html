Offset Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Overview

Offset is an incentive-estimation and claim-planning app. It does not require an account and does not sell personal data or use data for cross-app tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your ZIP code, utility selection, residence details, optional income, project inputs, saved projects, checklist progress, onboarding state, and notification preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this local data, subject to normal device backups managed by Apple.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription payments. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app-user identifier plus purchase and entitlement information needed to offer and restore Offset Premium. Offset does not receive payment-card details. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Optional notifications

If you enable notifications, OneSignal receives a randomly generated Offset identifier, the device's push subscription identifier, notification interactions, and two limited segmentation tags: lifecycle stage and Premium access status. Offset does not send your entered ZIP code, utility, residence type, income, sticker price, saved-project name, matched program details, estimated savings, deadlines, or checklist text to OneSignal. OneSignal's privacy policy is available at onesignal.com/privacy_policy.

Rebate data updates

Offset creates a random anonymous Supabase identifier without collecting your name, email address, or password. The same random identifier is used with RevenueCat so a protected server function can confirm an active Premium entitlement before delivering private incentive catalogs. These requests do not contain your entered ZIP code, utility, residence details, income, project inputs, saved projects, or checklist data. Supabase may process connection information such as an IP address and device network metadata under its privacy terms. Supabase’s privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Purposes and retention

Subscription data is used to provide Premium access. Notification data is used to deliver reminders and measure notification interactions. Supabase Storage is used only to distribute public catalog files. Third-party providers retain data according to their policies and contractual settings. You can stop future notification data use by disabling reminders in Offset or notifications in iOS Settings.

Choices

  • Notifications are optional and are requested only after their value is explained.
  • You can disable reminders in Offset or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can manage or cancel Premium from your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • You can delete locally stored Offset data by deleting the app.

Children

Offset is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect directly identifying information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Offset's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
